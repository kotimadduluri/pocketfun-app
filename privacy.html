Privacy policy

Pocket Fun, by kotimadduluri. Effective 4 October 2026.

This policy explains what the Pocket Fun app for Android and iOS does with your data. In short: your game data stays on your device, we have no servers and no accounts, and the only third party involved is Google, which serves the ads.

Data the app keeps on your device

To work, the app stores the following on your phone or tablet:

  • Game progress, so you can continue a game later.
  • Best scores.
  • Your settings.
  • Player names you type in for Ludo.
  • Usage stats kept on the device: games played, time played and results.

None of this is sent to us. We do not run servers, there are no accounts, and we cannot see or access this data.

Device backups

If you have your device's own backup turned on (Google's Android backup, or iCloud backup on iOS), that backup may include this app's data. It is stored under your own Google or Apple account under their terms, and we cannot access it.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob on screens outside a game. Ads are not shown while a game is in progress. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as device identifiers (for example your advertising ID), your IP address, and how you interact with ads.

Where required, the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, using Google's consent form. On iOS, Apple's tracking prompt asks whether the app may track you; you can change that choice later in your device settings.

To learn more, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy, which covers AdMob.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send analytics or usage data to us or to any analytics service.
  • It does not include a crash reporting service.
  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It has no in-app purchases.

Children

Pocket Fun is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

  • Use Delete all data in the app's Settings to remove the app's data from your device.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your device.
  • You can reset your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub. Issues there are public, so please do not include personal information.
