Classic games, offline, no account.

Five familiar games in one small app for Android and iPhone. Open it and play, with or without a signal.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store

The games

  • 2048

    Slide the tiles, merge matching numbers, and aim for 2048.

  • Snake

    Steer, eat, grow longer, and keep clear of your own tail.

  • Sudoku

    Fill the grid so every row, column and box holds 1 to 9.

  • Minesweeper

    Read the numbers, flag the mines, and clear the field.

  • Ludo

    Pass-and-play on one phone for 2 to 4 players. Fill any empty seat with a computer opponent.

How it works

Plays offline
Every game runs on your phone. No connection needed to play.
No account
No sign-up and no login. Your progress, scores and settings stay on your device.
No ads while you play
The app shows ads from Google AdMob, only on screens outside a game.
Your data, your call
Delete all data from Settings at any time. Read the privacy policy.
